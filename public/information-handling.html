Information handling.

Read this practical overview before sending personal information or confidential documents through a quotation enquiry.

What the quotation form requests

The form asks for an organization, contact person, email, optional phone number, procurement category, requirements and optional supporting documents. These details are submitted with your quotation request so the company can review your enquiry.

What happens to attachments?

Attachments are submitted with your enquiry and made available to authorised staff. Do not upload identification documents, bank details, medical records or other sensitive data unless Amantusi has specifically requested them through an appropriate secure process.

Local draft saving

Draft saving is opt-in. When enabled, draft form text is stored in this browser for up to seven days, not transmitted until submission. File attachments are not included in drafts. You can clear the draft using the form's Clear saved draft control. Other people using this browser may be able to access locally saved information.

Retention, access and rights

For information about how your enquiry is retained or to request access, correction or deletion of your details, contact us using the information below. Requests are considered in accordance with applicable law.
