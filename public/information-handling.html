Website information

Information handling.

Read this practical overview before sending personal information or confidential documents through a quotation enquiry.

What the quotation form requests

The form asks for an organization, contact person, email, optional phone number, procurement category, requirements and optional supporting documents. It sends these details to the website's server-side RFQ service for processing.

What happens to attachments?

Files are transmitted with the enquiry to configured website storage and made available through protected administrator workflows. Do not upload identification documents, bank details, medical records or other sensitive data unless Amantusi has specifically requested them through an appropriate secure process.

Local draft saving

Draft saving is opt-in. When enabled, draft form text is stored in this browser for up to seven days, not transmitted until submission. File attachments are not included in drafts. You can clear the draft using the form's Clear saved draft control. Other people using this browser may be able to access locally saved information.

Retention, access and rights

The website source does not specify a verified records-retention period or complete data-protection policy. To ask about access, correction, deletion or the current handling policy, contact the company using the details below. Applicable rights depend on the law and the nature of the record.
