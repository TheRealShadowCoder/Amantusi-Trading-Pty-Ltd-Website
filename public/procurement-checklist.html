Procurement resource

Before you send an RFQ.

Use this practical checklist to prepare a more complete enquiry. The form supports specifications and document attachments.

Submit a quotation request
01 / CHECK

Company details

Include your organisation or department, contact person, email and phone number.

02 / CHECK

Reference and deadline

Add the RFQ, tender or purchase order reference and required-by date.

03 / CHECK

Products and quantities

Specify precise product descriptions, dimensions, units, pack sizes and quantities.

04 / CHECK

Compliance needs

List required specifications, certifications or documentation explicitly; do not assume them.

05 / CHECK

Logistics

Provide complete delivery destination, access requirements and receiving instructions.

06 / CHECK

Supporting documents

Attach schedules, specifications and reference sheets; the RFQ form supports up to five files.

07 / CHECK

Alternative products

State whether an equivalent product may be offered and which characteristics are non-negotiable.

08 / CHECK

Commercial conditions

Add special delivery terms, quotation validity or procurement conditions where applicable.

09 / CHECK

Final review

Verify names, dates, quantities, attachments and contact information before submitting.
