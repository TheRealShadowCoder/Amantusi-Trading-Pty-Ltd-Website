Working with Amantusi

Supplier introductions, structured from day one.

Amantusi Trading welcomes relevant supplier introductions. This page describes a suggested first-contact package; it is not an automated registration or promise of supplier approval.

Prepare an RFQRFQ checklist

What to prepare.

A useful first introduction provides enough information to assess whether a follow-up discussion is relevant.

01 / REQUIRED CONTEXT

Business identification

Business or trading name, business address, contact person and contact details.

02 / REQUIRED CONTEXT

Supply categories

List product or service families and the regions your business can supply.

03 / REQUIRED CONTEXT

Product information

Describe product specifications, unit sizes, price basis and any minimum order quantities.

04 / REQUIRED CONTEXT

Logistics capabilities

Summarize lead-time estimates, dispatch locations and delivery arrangements.

05 / REQUIRED CONTEXT

Documentation on request

Keep statutory, product and banking verification documents ready for a verified, authorized request.

06 / REQUIRED CONTEXT

Commercial communication

State terms clearly. Do not email highly sensitive documents until a secure exchange method is agreed.

Contact pathway

Introduce your business.

Email a concise capability overview using the subject “Supplier Introduction — [Trading Name]”. Do not include personal identity or financial records in an unsolicited email.

What happens next

  • Submission does not guarantee appointment or registration
  • Availability and commercial terms are reviewed case by case
  • Never make payments to unknown contacts claiming to act as procurement intermediaries
