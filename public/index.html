Visit all frequently asked questions ↗
Can I attach a tender or specification file?

Yes. Attach up to five supported documents, each no larger than 10 MB, with a combined maximum of 30 MB. Only send documents relevant to your request.

How do I request catering pricing?

Browse the catering menu, select optional menu ideas, add your date, guest count, venue and dietary requirements, then continue to the quotation form.

Does sending an RFQ reserve stock?

No. Supply, prices, dates and delivery terms need separate confirmation. A submitted enquiry is not a confirmed purchase order.

Where can suppliers introduce themselves?

See our supplier information page for the initial introduction details and how to contact the business.
