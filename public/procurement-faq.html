How do I submit a quotation request?

Open Request a Quote from the homepage, enter your organization and contact details, describe the products or service and submit. A reference appears only after the server confirms receipt.

Can I attach an RFQ or tender schedule?

Yes. The website form supports up to five PDF, Office, CSV, text or supported image files, each up to 10 MB, with a maximum combined total of 30 MB.

Are prices confirmed on the catering menu?

No. Catering prices depend on guest count, menu selections, venue, staffing and delivery requirements. Request a tailored quotation.

Can I send a mixed-category sourcing enquiry?

Yes. Choose General Procurement and give quantities, specifications and delivery location for each requested item.

Does a quotation request guarantee stock or an order?

No. A submitted enquiry starts a review. Availability, lead time, final price and accepted purchase terms must be confirmed separately.

How can I contact the company?

Use the email or phone number on the company profile. For a complete procurement brief, the secure website enquiry form is preferred.

How do I prepare an accurate RFQ?

State item specifications, units, quantities, brands or permissible alternatives, delivery requirements and deadline. See the printable RFQ checklist.

Can I submit a catering brief without choosing a listed menu item?

Yes. Describe your custom catering needs, guest count, event date, location and service format when building the catering brief.
